# J Automotive Academy research board

## Interaction and lesson design consulted

- [Ciechanowski, Gears](https://ciechanow.ski/gears/) — teaches by making geometry and motion continuously respond to controls. The gear pair and piston labs use linked SVG dimensions plus live numerical readouts, rather than a static explainer illustration.
- [Explorable Explanations](https://explorabl.es/) — guided the small, direct manipulation pattern: one variable changes, an immediate visual consequence appears, and a question asks the learner to predict or interpret it.
- [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/) — informed the use of a visible prediction, worked example, learner-controlled experiment and immediate authored feedback.
- [IXL](https://www.ixl.com/math) — informed objective-level evidence, gradual difficulty bands and repeatable practice. The current version records progress locally and does not claim an external validated credential.
- [3Blue1Brown](https://www.3blue1brown.com/lessons/neural-networks) — informed the visual-first sequence: make one relationship visible at a time and keep labels attached to what they explain.
- [PhET](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/simulations/filter?subjects=physics&type=html) — informed clear model limits, accessible controls and direct manipulation. The labs label fixed-coefficient, schematic and timing assumptions.
- [Animagraffs](https://animagraffs.com/) — informed cutaway-style annotations and the use of spatial callouts. The current SVGs are schematic; they are not represented as manufacturing drawings.

## Frontend and animation resources

- [Anthropic frontend-design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design) and the locally installed web testing skill were consulted for browser-native layouts and practical interface verification.
- [UI/UX Pro Max](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) was queried for learning-platform and focus guidance. The generated generic showcase/clay style did not fit the project's editorial engineering identity, so the Academy uses a restrained drawing-paper, blueprint, ink and brass palette instead.
- [Remotion agent skills](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/agents/skills) were reviewed. Remotion is React/video-oriented while this project is a static vanilla-JS site and does not render video files; VibeVid is implemented as a user-controlled, transcript-visible browser storyboard, with no new framework dependency.

## Fact references used in the six demos

- [NASA Glenn, Drag Equation](https://www1.grc.nasa.gov/beginners-guide-to-aeronautics/drag-equation/) — fixed-input force model, speed-squared relationship.
- [U.S. Department of Energy, Diesel Engine Fundamentals](https://www.energy.gov/sites/default/files/2026-04/DOE-HDBK-1018-93_VOL1.pdf) — four-stroke sequence and cycle context; demo explicitly describes its clean timing intervals as simplified.
- [Ferrari, 90 years of Scuderia Ferrari](https://cdn.ferrari.com/cms/network/media/pdf/pr_90_years_eng_fin.pdf) — primary-source context for the F40 and anniversary year.
- [OpenStax, rotational motion](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/10-introduction), [unit conversion](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/1-6-unit-conversion), and [combustion chemistry](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-2-classifying-chemical-reactions) — supporting math, units and balanced octane model.
- [U.S. Energy Information Administration, gasoline](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/gasoline/) — supports the caveat that commercial gasoline is a blend, while octane is one representative molecule for atom-count instruction.

## Stack and rights decisions

The gallery is an existing static vanilla-JS bundle. The Academy uses inline/data-driven SVG and browser controls, reuses the existing local guest/JP ledger, and adds no service, database or third-party runtime. No remote automotive photograph was added. The F40 application image reuses the gallery's already credited local asset and links back to its gallery credit. Reference links are citations for learning, not copied prose or assets.

## Current scope

Six lessons are authored end to end; sixteen subjects and their skill maps are visible. Subjects without authored demos are explicitly identified as mapped/planned, not presented as finished courses. Question bands are rule-generated from reviewed answer keys, not generated by a live AI service.
